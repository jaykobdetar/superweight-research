## Novelty: narrow candidate additions, not a priority claim

Established research already describes super weights, massive activations, attention sinks and their training development. The original [novelty comparison](../NOVELTY.md), the developmental report's literature section and the [independent review](../../reviews/2026-10-22-independent/REPORT.md) remain required context. Discovering a large coefficient or a sink-like source is not claimed as new.

The additions here are exact-checkpoint, exact-intervention measurements: separated development of the named H12/H14 responses, specific alternate-writer decomposition, conditioned native-source dependence, and direct row-local Adam-state accounting. They are potentially useful causal/detail contributions, not proof of priority over all prior work. The full computational purpose and optimizer's causal role in formation are still hypotheses.

## Negative and unresolved results retained

Cross-time swaps can fail; source responses are not monotonic; the nominal secondary scalar's raw-coordinate bound does not imply a downstream role; whole-neuron deletion has natural counterexamples; near-constant replacements retain output errors; and a tiny second moment does not explain the primary's current update relative to other neurons in the sampled late output row. The separate within-neuron axis comparison does find a modest denominator advantage. None of these is dropped to simplify the story.
