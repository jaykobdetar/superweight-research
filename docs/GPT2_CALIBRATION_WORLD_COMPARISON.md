# Report 19 — calibration-world comparison

Read the [complete reviewed report and all 64 primary comparisons](../results/calibration_world_comparison_report/report19.md), or open its [self-contained HTML](../reports/report19.html). The [reproduction guide](REPRODUCE_GPT2_CALIBRATION_WORLD_COMPARISON.md) separates presentation rebuilding, public numerical reanalysis, input reconstruction and optional model replay.

This finite GPT-2 study compared interventions calibrated on the same four texts with source-token values removed or retained. Calibration changed manual gate-edit parameters; it did not train or change GPT-2 weights. Original frozen F supplies the value-lesioned parameters. A center × direction-pair design then compared PP, PC, CP and CC during otherwise native evaluation. Each cell included full removal/control proposals and early-query interventions matched to its own actual projected-write norm target.

The completed acquisition was 16 calibration calls followed by 1,160 evaluation calls: 576 MAIN, 576 rotated-SUFFIX controls and eight native/identity anchors. Two planned 580-call segments completed without a pretrained acquisition failure. The 16 evaluation passages yield 32 natural/BOS identities; BOS changes token 0 only. Four passage clusters per source, one fit, one declared orthogonal control and unequal natural cross-cell doses limit interpretation.

The calibration choice changed removal-minus-own-orthogonal-control later-loss contrasts by small amounts in this panel. Of 64 correlated nominal primary intervals, 51 included zero. The largest absolute mean endpoint shift, D_CC − D_PP, was about 0.0265 millinats per later target. These are conditional fixed-fit estimates, not an equivalence test, a population result, a ranking of intrinsic principal-component importance or evidence that calibration is irrelevant. All signs, intervals, passage values, secondary contrasts and paired-start rows remain available.

Mask-route diagnostics were byte-identical in all **128 paired stored-array comparisons of allocation logits and mass**, involving 256 array operands. This differs from the 64 PE arrays used in the earlier original-F continuity gate. Geometric similarities of PE/CE parameters are descriptive; native calibration can already differ upstream at layers 4–9.

Independent numerical, learned-projection and selected-readout audits passed for the original execution. Their public views are commitments with explicit scopes. Selected saved-logit precision checks do not bound unsaved rows or the numerical resolution of every tiny contrast. Public numerical reanalysis uses the published observations and frozen statistical helpers; it does not reconstruct omitted activations, predictions or model arithmetic.

The export excludes source text, token/prediction/target IDs, raw calibration allocation arrays, full activations, vocabulary rows, checkpoints, private configuration and failure payloads. Three reviewed aggregate fit/reference NPZs are permitted; they contain fixed-dimensional fit summaries, not per-position calibration observations. Infrastructure corrections are recorded in a [typed provenance summary](../reviews/calibration_world_comparison/review_summaries.json), separate from scientific hypotheses.

Established activation, attention-gating and GPT-2 sink mechanisms are credited in the full report's primary literature. The contribution here is the particular controlled calibration comparison. A control-direction ensemble is a proposed continuation, not an executed finding, approved schedule or part of this package.

Publication checkpoint: the original study, final report, metadata package and restricted numerical package have passed their independent reviews. Both public-only reanalysis and permitted-byte comparison passed. Tokenizer reconstruction and source-only staging also passed independent actual-binding review. These checks prepare commands; they do not execute a model replay. Package-specific commitments are recorded in the accompanying provenance summary. A fresh 16-call calibration replication, a new full 1,160-call evaluation replay, and a second complete T22 776-call replay are **unperformed**.
