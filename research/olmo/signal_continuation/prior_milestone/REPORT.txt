WHAT PRODUCES THE OLMo SUPER-WEIGHT SIGNAL?
Bounded causal-production investigation, 23 October 2026

Model: allenai/OLMo-1B-0724-hf
Exact revision: d7cbab742d80589e714b1a2d7f838dcd21cbe143
Repository reviewed: jaykobdetar/superweight-research at

All layer, head, neuron and coordinate indices are zero-based.

MAIN ANSWER

The dominant pulse is a history-dependent SwiGLU write, not a constant scalar
inserted independently of the rest of the network. Early attention changes the
input direction seen by the layer-1 MLP. The layer-0 MLP is a conditional carrier
of the newline-history effect, and layer-1 attention strongly amplifies it.
Neuron 1710's gate changes from negative to positive while its up factor becomes
strongly negative, producing a large negative output at coordinate 1764.

The new result is that neuron 1710 is not the only producer of the surviving
coordinate-1764 signal. Other layer-1 MLP neurons already make negative writes
in the same forward pass. After the entire 1710 output column is deleted, these
pre-existing writes, not mostly the incoming residual, supply the remaining
negative axis for four of the five tested source tokens. This is not adaptive
compensation: deleting a down-projection output does not change that layer's
already-computed neuron activations.

The alternate writers are context-dependent. Neuron 7579 is the largest negative
alternate writer for all five tested first-token states. Later H14-induced
newline states instead have leading contributions from neurons 3950 and 4786.
For a single newline at source position 0, substantial positive alternate writes
nearly cancel the negative ones. That exception leaves almost no negative axis
after whole-column deletion. It is a concrete local mechanism consistent with
the previously recorded newline-source column failures, not yet a demonstrated
behavioral rescue target.

1. WHAT WAS ACTUALLY RUN

The recovered evidence contained normalized inputs to the final checkpoint's
layer-1 MLP, but not its full matrices. Exactly three official pinned tensors
were acquired with verified HTTP byte ranges: layer-1 gate, up and down weights,
201,326,592 bytes total (192 MiB). No other layer, full checkpoint, GPU, training
run or downstream inference was acquired or executed.

The complete layer-1 MLP was replayed on 21 cached FP32 inputs:
- Five single-token source-position-0 states: The (510), period (15), newline (187),
  double-newline (535) and space (209).
- Four fixed synthetic prose panels, each with four q=128 newline conditions:
  no earlier newline, an earlier newline at p=64, the same earlier-newline world
  with only L0/H14 q-to-p blocked, and its H0 edge control.

The incoming centered residual was reconstructed from the cached normalized
direction and its recorded centered norm. Its mean is immaterial to the
non-affine LayerNorm geometry studied here. Full-column deletion zeroed neuron
1710's activation before a fresh down projection. Scalar-edge deletion replaced
only output coordinate 1764 with the corresponding no-1710 value. Neither means
deleting the entire down-projection row or changing the weight at every token.

All cached selected rows match the newly acquired matrices bitwise. Reconstructed
known contributions have maximum scaled error 4.12e-7; normalized-state recovery
has maximum absolute error 1.98e-6. Direct exclusion avoids the roughly 1.88e-4
roundoff possible when subtracting a dominant write from a rounded full output.
Peak analysis RSS was about 233 MiB. An independent FP64 audit checked provenance,
all 21 states, 189 masked-neuron interventions, 147 geometry conditions, 16
gate/up factorials and 16 carry/other-MLP swaps. No substantive defect was found.

This is an extension of the existing NumPy prefix computation, not an independent
native-framework or full-model replication. The original public experiments use
FP16; new local replay results below use the pinned FP32 weights and FP32 cache.

2. THE EARLY CAUSAL PATHS MUST REMAIN DISTINCT

H14: prior-newline history

The public controlled experiment changes a p=64 space (209) to newline (187) while
keeping q=128 newline (187) and the suffix fixed. L0/H14's mass on p changes from
roughly .001 to .94-.96. Blocking only that edge restores the later pulse; H0
and sham-edge controls do not. In a no-prior-newline recipient, earlier-newline
donor K and V jointly suppress the pulse; either alone is insufficient. Thus
redirecting attention and supplying the appropriate value content both matter.

The public 28-case component factorial was reanalyzed from its 448 rows. At q,
the residual before the layer-1 MLP is embedding + A0 + M0 + A1. Here A0 is the
layer-0 attention output, M0 its MLP output, and A1 layer-1 attention output.
- With the other two components frozen clean, the M0 donor induces a pulse
  from -75.92 to -124.41. A0 alone or A1 alone does not.
- M0+A1 donor outputs induce -131.60 to -311.10.
- Swapping only M0 while leaving later A1 endogenous induces -131.92 to -308.26.
- Restoring clean M0 in the edge-blocked world suppresses the pulse to 0-.00220.
- The finite M0-by-A1 factorial interaction ranges from -197.88 to -55.69,
  median -188.33. This is a conditional interaction, not a mediation percentage.
All factorial interaction closures are exact, and a local FP16 scalar-factor
replay reproduces all 448 recorded pulse values exactly.

In the four newly replayed endpoint panels, the suppressed primary gate is
-10.71 to -10.20, versus +21.17 to +21.32 after H14-edge removal. The up factor
moves from -4.43..-2.07 to -23.28..-22.55. The normalized input norms change only
about 0.5%, whereas their directions change substantially. Positive rescaling
alone cannot reverse a gate's sign; this is not merely an input-norm effect.

A neuron-local 2x2 factor replay makes the proximate interaction explicit:
- Suppressed gate + edge-world up: +.00343 to +.00558, still no negative pulse.
- Edge-world gate + suppressed up: -59.35 to -27.86, partial negative write.
- Both edge-world factors: -311.60 to -302.87.
The gate is necessary for the large negative write in these donor combinations;
the up change strongly amplifies it. These are scalar-node interventions, not
proof that all factors can be independently induced by natural prefix inputs.

H12: empty early-attention history

This is a different intervention. A later The/period token is made self-only in
the first two attention layers, rather than changing an earlier newline edge.
The public 64-case H12 experiment was checked separately:
- Ordinary gates are negative; isolated gates are approximately +14.78.
- The isolated H12 context transplanted into the ordinary world induces a large
  pulse; the reciprocal ordinary H12 context suppresses the isolated pulse.
- Replacing H12 context by the current source position 0 value exactly reproduces the
  isolated-donor pulse. Replacing it by the current boundary value, or merely
  making that H12 query self-only, does not reproduce the negative pulse.
- The latter two controls agree exactly, as do the source position 0-value/donor controls.
- Swapping both scalar gate/up factors exactly reconstructs the isolated pulse.
  Up alone leaves it near zero; gate alone is much smaller and sometimes has the
  wrong sign because the ordinary up factor can be positive.

This identifies a feature-content-and-routing interaction. It does not identify
H12 as the relay for H14 newline suppression; the prior reciprocal H12 swaps
failed to transfer that newline state. The two phenomena cannot be conflated.

3. WHERE THE SURVIVING SIGNAL COMES FROM

Let P center a residual vector, and let e be the unit vector at coordinate 1764.
Define the unit centered axis u = P e / sqrt(1-1/2048). For neuron j, its signed
axis write is a_j * (Wdown[1764,j] - mean(Wdown[:,j])) / sqrt(1-1/2048).
The post-MLP source is the incoming centered residual plus these neuron writes.

After the whole 1710 column is removed:

Token                 Incoming axis    Other-MLP axis    Total axis    Alignment
The (510)                    -0.6154          -9.3000          -9.9154       -.8441
Period (15)                  -0.3830          -5.3776          -5.7605       -.7299
Newline (187)                +0.0192          -0.1238          -0.1046       -.0097
Double-newline (535)         -0.3895          -8.7687          -9.1583       -.8529
Space (209)                  -0.6536          -6.6349          -7.2885       -.7610

For the four non-single-newline examples, other neurons supply 91.0-95.7% of the
signed remaining negative axis. These are signed decomposition shares, not
nonnegative causal fractions or percentages of model performance. They are
unstable when positive and negative components cancel.

For The (510), the largest alternate axis writes are 7579(-3.9293), 7845(-1.7096),
6947(-1.5685) and 2637(-1.1212). Neuron7579 alone is not necessary for a negative
alignment: deleting it after 1710 leaves The at -.6981 and period at -.3186.
Retaining only 7579 plus the incoming residual is sufficient for a negative
alignment around -.74 to -.81 across these five states, but not for faithful
source vectors, later K/V, or useful model behavior. No unique minimal circuit
has been established.

At q=128 after H14-edge removal, the surviving axis is -9.72 to -11.38; 98.5-99.7%
of that signed axis comes from other MLP neurons. The leading pair 3950/4786
accounts for about 68.6-76.5% of the other-neuron net axis. Their activation signs
and roles differ from those at source position 0. A universal fixed backup set is therefore
not supported.

With 1710 absent, swapping only the edge-world other-MLP output into the suppressed
recipient yields alignment -.728 to -.766. Swapping only the incoming residual
does not: alignment remains about -.017 to -.041. This is conditional sufficiency
for the local source geometry, not a demonstrated downstream rescue.

4. WHY DELETING MORE CAN DAMAGE LESS

The primary weight accounts for 97.9049% of its column's squared norm, but the
remaining 2.0951% is multiplied by a very large activation. Scalar-edge deletion
leaves this large off-axis complement intact. Whole-column deletion removes it.

For source The (510):
- Native: axis -268.46, orthogonal norm 37.66, alignment -.9903.
- Delete only (1764,1710): axis about -10.00, orthogonal norm 37.66, alignment -.2567.
- Delete all 1710 outputs: axis -9.915, orthogonal norm 6.30, alignment -.8441.

The remaining negative coordinate barely changes between the two lesions; the
large change is the orthogonal contamination and therefore LayerNorm direction.
This clarifies why a small squared-weight complement need not be small relative
to the residual left after the dominant contribution is removed.

The public full-model experiments already establish that the distorted source
is subsequently used through coupled K/V pathways: column-world source K in a
scalar-deleted recipient nearly restores the tested controlled losses, whereas
the corresponding V-only replacement does not. Geometry-donor experiments also
show that alignment and orthogonal direction interact. The new replay ends at
the source state that feeds L2 normalization; it does not recompute any K/V map,
attention probabilities or downstream losses.

5. THE SINGLE-NEWLINE EXCEPTION IS CANCELLATION

For source 187, alternate negative writes sum to -7.91913 and positive writes to
+7.79537. Their net is only -.12376. Adding incoming +.01918 leaves -.10459.
Thus alternate negative writers are present, but are almost canceled.

The positive columns 2476 (+2.34633), 2637 (+1.42561), 7845 (+1.28574) contribute much
of this opposition. Removing these three full columns, in addition to 1710,
changes local source alignment from -.00973 to -.65124. This surgical result
demonstrates a local cancellation mechanism. It has not been run through later
layers, so it must not be described as perplexity rescue.

The independent computational-role investigation verified by exact source and
token hashes that the original problematic DuBois source position 0 window begins token 187.
Its public column-deleted L2 alignment is -.0096, consistent with this FP32 replay.
Across its broader 32-window join, all three source position 0 token 187 cases have recorded
whole-column perplexity ratios 7.03-10.62. This is convergent evidence, not proof
that the three-column intervention above fixes those cases. In particular, a
later newline with a real prefix has different alternate writers and must not
be treated as equivalent to a source position 0 newline.

6. THE CORRECT SECOND PAPER COORDINATE

The checked coordinate is layer 1, output1764, neuron 8041. For ideal non-affine
LayerNorm input h, sum(h)=0 and ||h||^2<=2048. Center the gate/up rows g,v.
Because |SiLU(g.h)| <= |g.h| and the symmetric bilinear form has spectral radius
(||g||||v||+|g.v|)/2,

|w SiLU(g.h)(v.h)| <= |w| * 1024 * (||g||||v|| + |g.v|).

The newly acquired full matrices independently reproduce 0.16032984931200406.
This is a prompt-independent real-arithmetic bound on the RAW coordinate 1764
write, not a sampled maximum, floating-point-certified theorem, or bound on
downstream importance. The analogous centered-axis bound is 0.16069531; the
whole-column L2 bound is 25.72159. It would be incorrect to infer that neuron 8041
is globally negligible from the scalar bound.

7. WHAT REMAINS UNPROVEN AND THE NEXT DISCRIMINATING TEST

The work now traces an upstream history-dependent gate and several concrete
alternate writers into the immediate source geometry. It does not identify
every necessary layer-0 MLP neuron or complete the downstream behavioral circuit.
Selected alternate sets are exploratory and were chosen after looking at these
same states. No held-out selection claim, training-causal claim or universal
token generalization is justified.

The next useful test is not another scalar-value sweep. On a separate frozen
panel, run paired native and whole 1710-deleted recipients with:
1. Alternate-writer group removal and exact same-world add-back controls.
2. For source 187, remove the positive trio or patch only its net axis while
   preserving matched orthogonal/norm controls. Test whether loss is rescued.
3. Patch the resulting L2 source K and V separately and jointly, then distinguish
   early transmission from later dynamic query effects. Keep source position,
   scoring span and target IDs fixed; report per-window failures.
4. For the upstream path, localize which layer-0 MLP features cause the gate
   rotation using component/feature interventions, not weight-magnitude sweeps.

A downstream result requires later weights and a validated full-model execution
or blockwise equivalent. Those resources were not acquired under this bounded
extension. The existing work can nominate exact interventions, but cannot claim
their behavioral outcomes before that test is run.
