# What was tried before the literary test

These stages used one checkpoint, often reusing windows to separate specific mechanisms. They are not independent replications. H0 below denotes layer 2 head 0; H14 denotes layer 0 head 14. “Nominated edit” is an artificial perturbation in a direction chosen by a reader, not removal of that reader or of the dominant scalar. Scalar and whole-writer removals are position-local residual-contribution interventions.

| Stage | Question and controls | Result and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| B/B2, T1 | Do size-, mean- and radial-matched directions nominated by H0 produce extra downstream KL over shuffled directions or the other 15 real heads? | Initial newline: 8/8 positive over shuffled directions, median excess 1.336 nats; only 6/8 exceeded the real-head gate (ratio ≥1.25 and excess ≥0.001), H0 never ranked first. Across word starters the real-head threshold passed 2/8. |
| C1 | Cross four suffixes with newline and four new word starters; use identical directions and fixed dose. | Newline's relative H0 excess was larger on all four suffixes but passed the numerical interaction screen on 2/4 (3 required). Absolute H0 KL was smaller on newline. |
| R1–R3 | Restore true layer-2 source value heads after an H0-nominated perturbation; test H0 alone, complement alone and all together, then H8 nomination as crossover. | H0 alone worsened KL, all values recovered ~41–44%. The H0 marginal switched sign with the other 15 values clean (3/3 source groups); H0-nomination specificity transferred to new suffixes (3/3) but the symmetric H8 gate passed only 1/3. Not a unique explanation of the actual scalar lesion. |
| I1, D1 | At naturally occurring query newlines in eight stdlib windows, compare true scalar, whole writer and nominated direction; substitute an earlier newline, then transplant only the writer dose. | Original scalar vs whole median KL 2.066 vs 0.00518. H0 switch gate 0/8 for true scalar and whole. Earlier newline suppressed writer and scalar KL; putting the strong donor amplitude into that background restored median scalar KL to 2.785 (8/8). A new off-distribution donor state, not evidence for exclusive mediation. |
| H1 | Replace the same earlier slot with space, tab, semicolon or single newline. Test reciprocal, source-query-only L0 context swaps for all heads. | Only newline suppressed the writer in all eight. H14 ranked first on both legs throughout; restored median lesion KL 2.609, reverse median 0.0000026. The project's earlier [H14 finding](../../../docs/REPORT.md#2-a-preceding-newline-suppresses-a-later-pulse) predates this study. |
| G1 | Four new legal texts and four READMEs; distance 8/24, common substitutions plus reciprocal true-head donor tests. | 7/8 documents passed joint functional gate at each distance. The same coreutils example missed the reciprocal KL bound twice despite 97–98% activation suppression. |
| U1 | Compare H14 donor directions controlling write length, mean, and projections onto both recipient residuals. | Overmatching: only 3/8 files had enough unconstrained direction to qualify. 0/3 qualified files passed specificity. Matching both recipients partly preserves their difference; the null is not evidence that size alone suffices. |
| U2 | Freeze the mean H14 context difference from eight code files; compare to eight fixed equal-post-projection-length directions on eight entirely different READMEs/FAQs. | 8/8 met both directional and conditional behavioral gates. Adding the vector to earlier-newline history produced median lesion KL 1.593 vs 0.0000053 random. Reversing its sign in the original history gave 0.0000056 vs 1.479 random. One amplitude, no target-specific fit. |
| N1 | Unmodified raw technical documents with a naturally preceding newline. Same-slot token substitution and natural alphabetic neighbor as controls. | 8/8 newline queries met joint gate, 0/8 of each alphabetic control. Two neighboring tokens had a behavioral effect without a large writer excursion. |
| L1, E1 | Raw literary text; compare intact shift (C), conditional lesion effect (V), and combined shift (J) over predeclared doses. | [Results including exceptions and non-monotonicity](README.md). |

**Technical stops:** The first native attempt stopped on an optional vision-library import before loading the model; an isolated, already-installed text runtime passed geometry checks. The first word-transfer attempt stopped on serialization before persisting its first row; a fresh attempt fixed boolean serialization only. I1 wrote all 16 per-context records then failed during its aggregate export; its aggregate was reconstructed and its natural diagonal later replayed in D1/H1. These are not additional successful runs.
