## Remaining scientific and packaging limits

This is one checkpoint/backend with small, often reused context panels. Bootstrap intervals are conditional on calibration and do not correct the adaptive research process. There is no independent researcher, hardware or checkpoint replication yet. The primary report's modest claims fit those limits.

Exact external inputs must be retrieved separately; changed upstream dataset packaging can prevent exact hash reproduction. The private repository deliberately omits model weights, corpora, token sequences and large raw activation tensors. Earlier historical pipelines are not all wired into the nine-batch runner. These limits are explicit rather than concealed behind a universal reproducibility claim.

Publication readiness does not establish transfer to other models, a unique minimal circuit, complete causal understanding, or a faithful deployment replacement. Subsequent transfer work should preserve the reported controls and record negative results.
