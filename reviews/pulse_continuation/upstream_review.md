Runtime flags cover complete sequence loss/prediction equality; independent saved-output replay covers the retained targets. Every isolated source/boundary intermediate matches exactly, every source0 intermediate remains unchanged across interventions, and boundary deletion leaves its own pre-projection trace unchanged. Target IDs, prefix crops, and both source identities independently match saved inputs.

All 256 development screen fractions reconstruct from saved pulse amplitudes, known scalar weight 0.63232421875, and donor intermediates. Ranking and random sets reproduce exactly. L1/H12 has mean signed gap fraction 1.81112; the next-ranked head L1/H4 has only 9.30e-7. Thus the remaining singleton ranks distinguish very small direct pulse effects, rather than identifying comparably important independent transmitters. The selected one-head patch overshoots the isolated pulse, so its gap fraction must not be described as accuracy or percentage faithfully recovered.

Across the sixteen held-out corpus/identity/prefix groups, mean gap fractions are 1.29452–1.94529 for isolated H12 implanted in ordinary history. The reverse transplant suppresses the isolated pulse almost to its ordinary level (fractions −6.19e-5 to 3.34e-6). Adding selected channels is strongly nonmonotonic: top2 yields 0.41150–0.84352 and top8 −0.00558–0.17684. Full L0 and full L1 patches alone yield 0.07493–0.21696 and 0.00591–0.15448 respectively, while all32 restores exactly 1. This is substantial context-dependent interaction; it is not an additive allocation of causal importance.

The random controls all contain L1/H12. Overlap counts with top sets are 1/1, 1/2, 2/4, 4/8 and 8/16. Control1 equals top1 and their complete retained outputs match exactly. No superiority claim over an H12-absent random-control family follows. Larger-set differences concern additional co-patched channels. Explicit absent-head controls belong in a separately labeled follow-up, preserving this screen and these frozen results.

Deleting the boundary scalar in the ordinary world changes corpus/identity/prefix mean loss by only −0.000156 to +0.000076 nats. After isolation, deletion adds 4.31336–5.99765 nats; after top2 induction it adds 4.34424–6.08785 nats. Top8 deletion effects span 0.000325–5.81065 nats. These are conditional dependencies on the boundary contribution in altered states. The original source0 contribution is intact, and head interventions also modify features beyond this scalar; neither sole mediation nor natural single-head necessity is established. Four windows per group support only limited pointwise uncertainty estimates.
