## Closest precedent: Weight-Dictionary Decomposition

[Ronish Bhatt, *Weight-Dictionary Decomposition: Reading a Transformer's Residual Stream with Its Own Writes*](https://github.com/sunmoonron/weight-dictionary-decomposition) is a September 2026 preprint. Its public repository explicitly maps section 4.5 to the channel-447 circuit. The [OpenReview PDF](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=pFniEnWkYR) returned a browser-verification page, and the author/DOI PDF could not be opened through the browsing tool. I therefore do **not** claim to have read that complete PDF. The following exact intervention facts are verified directly in the author's public primary implementation, not inferred from a search snippet:

- [`exp_s8_loop.py`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sunmoonron/weight-dictionary-decomposition/33504a4d90840bc01d0ef75609265e48a34403bb/scripts/exp_s8_loop.py), lines 62–97: scales channel 447 by 0.5/1.5 at the GPT-2 block-2 output and removes named regulator neurons at blocks 3–6. The hooks affect every token, and the script measures downstream coordinates, activations, and cross-entropy.
- [`exp_t2_compensation.py`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sunmoonron/weight-dictionary-decomposition/33504a4d90840bc01d0ef75609265e48a34403bb/scripts/exp_t2_compensation.py), lines 45–114: captures attention and MLP contributions by layer after regulator ablation and identifies changed neuron writes. Thus identifying downstream MLP compensation in this coordinate is already a specific precedent, beyond generic Hydra/self-repair.
- [`exp_u1_reserves.py`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sunmoonron/weight-dictionary-decomposition/33504a4d90840bc01d0ef75609265e48a34403bb/scripts/exp_u1_reserves.py), lines 56–90: crosses channel-447 boundary scaling with regulator ablation and tracks selected reserve neurons. This explicitly studies conditional responses rather than only native activity.
- [`exp_u2_leash.py`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sunmoonron/weight-dictionary-decomposition/33504a4d90840bc01d0ef75609265e48a34403bb/scripts/exp_u2_leash.py), lines 55–103: removes a regulator's full output, only its channel-447 contribution, or its non-447 complement, including a single-neuron control. Its [saved results](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sunmoonron/weight-dictionary-decomposition/main/results/exp_u2_leash.json) report differing reserve responses. These are prior coordinate/complement interventions; the general distinction cannot be presented as newly introduced here.

I inspected these hooks and saved results but did not independently reproduce that work. References to its reported compensation percentages should remain attributed, not be used as independently established facts in our report.

Our difference: the completed E/A/M experiment jointly zeroes raw coordinates 138/447 **only at source 0**, then selectively prevents later attention/MLP outputs from writing those coordinates, while leaving other coordinates and later positions endogenous. It measures losses on targets 2..511, raw source trajectories, natural/BOS starts and four corpora. The layer extension blocks whole coordinate-output writes by layer rather than removing the predecessor's named regulator neurons. These distinctions define our estimand; they do not make the underlying compensation idea new. The prior complement intervention also requires narrowing the general novelty wording in the OLMo section of `docs/NOVELTY.md`; the checkpoint-specific catastrophic row-versus-column and incompatible K/V findings remain separate empirical claims.

## Communication Map

[Richard Zhe Wang, *The Communication Map of a Transformer*, inspected arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.22007), section 5, pages 7–9 and Table 3, explicitly uses GPT-2 dimensions **447 and 138** as its outlier baseline. It removes selected directions at every layer input and before unembedding. Table 3 reports GPT-2 outlier deletion destroying 57.5% of induction gain and adding 4.2 nats on Pile, with random-subspace controls. It distinguishes these directions from its reader–writer PCA plane. This directly precedes using the exact pair as causal targets. Its intervention is repeated whole-stream deletion, whereas ours is one source-position lesion and selective subsequent writes; the losses and induction outcomes are not interchangeable. Neither the exact pair nor the basic idea of repeated coordinate suppression is new here.

## Other close papers

[Ran-Milo, Ofek and Mendel, *A Mechanistic Account of Attention Sinks in GPT-2*, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.14722v1), section 3.2.5–3.2.6 and Appendix A.2, names **138, 378 and 447**, connects first-position massive coordinates to the MLP-transformed positional signal and key/query-bias alignment, and tests sink-related interventions. Credit this specific positional/sink circuit, not only the earlier general massive-activation paper. Our current erasure/writer family does not test that complete circuit, and loss changes alone do not establish attention-sink mediation. Keep raw-coordinate, positional, and attention-sink mechanisms distinct.

[Li Hengyu, *Transient Reserves, Sink Dampers, and the Failure of Eigenvalue Reasoning in the Attention Propagator*, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.09279v1), section 7 and Figure 5, repeatedly clamps three massive dimensions at residual boundaries, including GPT-2's 447/378/138. It reports substantial loss costs in GPT-2, Pythia and Llama-3-8B, but sink collapse only in Llama. Its architectural interpretation is qualified by whole-stream scope and eight sequences. This is close prior art for architecture-dependent clamping effects; our checkpoint comparison does not isolate normalization type as their cause, and raw-coordinate dependence need not entail sink dependence.

[McClendon, Gallego-Feliciano and Saravanos, *The Life Cycle of a Massive Activation*, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00423v2), sections 4.3–4.7, distinguishes individual coordinates from collective RMS, uses matched-magnitude/direction perturbations, and reports redundant channel effects in Pythia. Its perturb-and-restore/training dynamics are a different setting from fixed-weight within-forward source regrowth. Nevertheless, generic redundancy and magnitude-versus-identity explanations already have direct controls in prior work. Our uniformly sampled random coordinate pair is not magnitude matched, and the E-rescale result cannot isolate a specific geometry mechanism.

## Suggested report/repository wording

Add the following substantive qualification near the existing self-repair discussion, with links to the primary sources above:

> Exact-coordinate precedents are particularly close. Earlier work already identifies GPT-2 dimensions 138/447 as intervention targets, studies channel-447 responses at the block-2/3 boundary, traces compensating attention/MLP writes, and separates a neuron's channel contribution from its complementary outputs. Our experiments are a targeted extension in intervention scope: source-0-only joint erasure crossed with later coordinate-write suppression, fresh paired loss evaluation, and a matched cross-model source-K/V study. We do not claim discovery of these coordinates, coordinate regulation, the coordinate/complement distinction, or general self-repair. The layer-local follow-up tests which permitted writes matter under this particular lesion family; it does not identify a unique native repair circuit.

In `docs/NOVELTY.md`, retain the specifically measured OLMo H14 history/K/V interaction and model-specific row/column outcome descriptions, but explicitly distinguish empirical extensions from invention of the intervention decomposition. Replace the prior search-based negative statement with: “A later exact-coordinate search found close precedents in both the target coordinates and intervention families; these are credited here. The remaining contribution is the scoped comparative dataset and its causal controls, without a priority claim.”

No change to a quantitative result is indicated by this review. No experiment launch blocker arises from this literature alone. Strong novelty/architecture/unique-mechanism claims would remain blocked until supported by additional evidence; the present report can be ready with explicit attribution and limits.
