## Motivation and competing accounts

T20 changed all nonsource L10 attention writes, then split readouts. Its Wiki/ Ethics early-versus-later loss reversals do not tell us which edited write positions produced the later effects. With native source values, distinguish effects caused by early writes through the remaining L11 attention block from effects requiring edits within the later region. Both can coexist, and nonlinear output/loss can create interaction. This is not a test of PC1 uniqueness; one fixed orthogonal direction is retained for comparability, with a direction ensemble a separate follow-up.

## Causal and numerical controls

- Installed identity equals native baseline at all512 logits and relevant tensors; all delivered inactive-query writes and q0 are byte-identical to baseline, including suffix runs.
- Later-only cannot alter earlier outputs in a causal model: require exact baseline prefix logits q0..126, plus early hidden/write equality, without changing kernel shapes or masks.
- Early-only must leave later L10 block outputs and incoming L11 later Q/K/V byte-identical to baseline. This establishes that later effects, if any, pass through earlier-position information in L11 attention rather than a changed later query or L10 later MLP. Save exact guards and bounded sampled raw evidence; no claim to separate earlier keys from values yet.
- Confirm early-only later L10 post-attention/MLP equality and every support union/copy rule. Later-only may affect subsequent later queries through L11 K/V; do not call it a purely query-local condition.
- Use unchanged fixed norm/RMS and per-query rounding bounds on active matched writes; report both domains and pointwise residual mismatch. Check source/inputs/weights/F/precision/failure journaling and suffix-prefix controls as T20. No tolerance tuning after outcomes.
- A nonzero early-only later effect identifies a controlled cross-position consequence through the remaining attention block under these guards, not a K/V mediation fraction, unique head/circuit, single mechanism or population benefit. Finite interaction can reflect nonlinear loss/readout; zero interaction does not imply no transmission.
