# GPT-2 source restoration versus alternative source features

Declared after the fresh grouped-contribution experiment. Removing three leading L2/r447 contributions and even its entire MLP output coordinate produces mild loss changes. Removing residual coordinates138 and447 afterL2 has a modest effect; removing the full source residual is much more harmful. Downstream RMS and normalized coordinate summaries change, but cannot by themselves show that the raw coordinates regenerate. This experiment measures the full source vectors and tests later writes directly.

The two feature coordinates and onset layer are frozen from the original development ranking and prior intervention definitions: r447 is the selected row, r138 is the strongest different row in that ranking, selected blockL2. Full2×2×2 factorial: E erases the two source0 residual coordinates immediately afterL2; A zeros attention-output writes to those source0 coordinates at each L3..11; M zeros MLP-output writes to those coordinates at each L3..11. All other component coordinates and positions remain unchanged by each hook, while downstream computation remains endogenous. E/A/M combinations distinguish conditional restoration from broader sensitivity to these writing operations. They do not identify a minimal neuronal circuit or additively decompose total causal effects. Capture full source residual vectors at all12 blocks and attention/MLP output vectors at L3..11, before/after for every condition. Verify exact zeroed coordinates and source-only localization. With E=A=M=1, both raw residual coordinates must remain zero after each later block; this is an expected arithmetic closure.

Controls: repeat the E=A=M=1 operation on two frozen uniform-random rows from the original controls; use E alone followed by rescaling the remaining source vector to its pre-erasure norm (no later writing clamp); and zero the entire source residual afterL2 as a broad lesion reference. The rescale condition changes magnitude while preserving the remaining direction; it does not imply LayerNorm makes every later residual operation scale-invariant. Use repeated-native and identity-hook anchors at the first natural input per corpus. Native/dropout precision and parameters remain unchanged.

A raw coordinate's return after erasure is evidence of restoration; a change in performance when a specific writer family is disabled is causal evidence of dependence on those operations in that recipient. A large coordinate can regenerate without being necessary for the tested task. Report negative cases, per-window interactions on NLL scale, KL/agreement, and uncertainty.
